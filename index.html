01 about

I started out shelving books and editing library web pages at Butler Community College. That job led to database work at Wichita State, and database work led to NIAR, where I now spend most days between SQL Server, Python and the Power Platform.

My work sits where research data meets the people who need it. Engineers want material properties without writing queries. Researchers want clean, comparable datasets without a week of spreadsheet cleanup. I build the layer in between.

Outside the day job I like game development, which is how I ended up writing C# and shipping Unity apps driven by research data.
